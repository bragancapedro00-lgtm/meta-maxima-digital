# Surface brief: landing page (src/app/page.tsx)

Scope: single-route paid-traffic landing page. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: SMB owners, local businesses and school owners arriving from Meta/Instagram Ads, mostly on phones.
Job: understand that the problem is the path from click to sale, trust the agency's strategic clarity, fill the qualification form.
Action: qualification form at the end -> pre-filled WhatsApp message (visitor confirms in-app).
Proof: no testimonials or metrics exist; trust is earned by diagnostic precision, honesty and transparency. Visuals use labelled conceptual data only.
Constraints: brief pins light ground (#FFFFFF/#F7F9FC), graphite text #111827, blue #2563EB/#1D4ED8/#EFF6FF, borders #E5E7EB, secondary #64748B, Inter or Geist, full section order and copy, hero badge "MARKETING E CRESCIMENTO EMPRESARIAL".
Build path this session: code-led (the hero composition is an interface diagram drawn by code; no raster art required). Not stored as a default.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the path to the sale. One blue conversion line starts in the hero's pipeline map, threads through the method and ends at the form. It refuses the agency template: gradient hero with rocket art, equal three-card grids, testimonial carousels, fake dashboards.

OWN-WORLD: A commercial planning document. Off-white #F7F9FC paper with white planes, graphite Geist display set tight (-0.03em), 1px #E5E7EB hairlines that divide content like a strategy map, blue reserved for the line, the signal and the primary action. Geist Mono only for stage labels and measurement-like annotations.

STORY: Visitor recognises the leak between click and sale, sees an integrated method that covers the whole path, understands the low-risk first step (a strategic conversation), fills the form and confirms in WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 64px header. Left 6/12: badge, H1 at ~4.25rem with "vender mais" and "acompanhar" in blue, subtitle, three benefit lines, primary + secondary buttons, microcopy. Right 6/12: "Mapa comercial" panel, a pipeline Anúncio -> Lead -> Atendimento -> Proposta -> Venda with a travelling blue signal and a flagged bottleneck, labelled "Visualização conceitual".

FORM: Brief-pinned direction (editorial strategy-map), first and only candidate. seed: none (brief-pinned)

Signature interaction: the travelling signal in the hero map, echoed when the four-step flow line draws on scroll. Motion grammar: exponential ease-out, transform/opacity/clip-path, disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
