# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript, vanilla CSS (CSS Modules + global tokens), static export (`output: "export"`). Chosen by the user. No UI or icon libraries; icons are authored SVG. Analytics IDs arrive through `NEXT_PUBLIC_*` environment variables.

## Users

- Owners of schools and educational institutions who need more enrollments.
- Owners and managers of small and medium businesses and local businesses that need to sell more.
- Companies that already pay for ads but fail to turn leads into sales.
- Businesses that need to structure marketing, customer service and sales processes.

They arrive mostly from Meta Ads / Instagram Ads, on a phone, with little time and some skepticism toward agencies.

## Product Purpose

Meta Máxima Digital is a marketing and business-growth agency. The landing page captures qualified leads, identifies each lead's commercial need, presents the agency's solutions, builds trust through strategic clarity, and hands the qualified lead to a commercial conversation on WhatsApp.

Success = a visitor completes the qualification form and confirms the pre-filled message inside WhatsApp.

## Positioning

Marketing that does not end at the click: paid traffic, sales strategy and technology (automation / AI) treated as one path from ad to sale. Operational experience in the education sector is a specialization, not the whole company.

## Operating Context

- Lead hand-off is WhatsApp (`wa.me`) with a pre-filled, structured message. Opening WhatsApp is not proof of a sent message; the visitor must confirm inside the app.
- Commercial WhatsApp: `5521984393482` (confirmed by the user).
- Campaign parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid) must be recognised and preserved.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services: paid traffic (Meta Ads and relevant channels, creatives, CPL and lead-quality analysis, continuous optimisation); sales strategy (journey analysis, bottlenecks, conversion, lead follow-up, process improvement); automation and AI (automated service flows, lead qualification, channel/CRM integration when applicable); digital strategy and creatives (ad creatives, graphic design, content planning, offer communication, brand consistency).

No pricing is published; scope is defined per company. No financial result can be promised.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Meta Máxima Digital" (text logotype "META MÁXIMA DIGITAL").
- A logo file exists but has not been supplied yet; the header and favicon must be ready to receive it. Never invent a symbol.
- Voice: strategic, sober, results-oriented, honest about uncertainty. Portuguese (pt-BR).

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. No testimonials, case studies, client logos, metrics, CNPJ, address, Instagram or other social URLs, privacy policy page. Future work must not fabricate any of them. Any data shown in visuals must be labelled illustrative.

## Product Principles

1. Truth over hype: no invented numbers, testimonials, scarcity or guarantees.
2. Every section moves the visitor toward the qualification form.
3. Diagnose before prescribing: the offer is a strategic conversation, not a package.
4. Transparency about data: say exactly what happens with the form and that WhatsApp needs confirmation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard-operable form and accordion, visible focus, labels above fields, Portuguese validation messages, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, comfortable touch targets on 360px phones.
